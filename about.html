About Us

Built on level ground.

Floor leveling done without leaving your home or business buried in dust.

Denver Dustless Floor Leveling was founded on a simple idea: floor leveling shouldn't leave your home or business covered in dust. So we built our entire process around containment — sealed work zones, vacuum-shrouded tools, and meticulous cleanup.

Why "dustless"? Traditional grinding and prep can fill a space with fine concrete dust that gets into everything. Our system protects your air, your belongings, and your timeline — so we can work in occupied homes and active businesses without shutting you down.

Every job gets the same standard: a properly assessed substrate, the right leveling system, and a floor that's genuinely flat when we leave.
