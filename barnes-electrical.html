Our Story

Colorado's Premier Electrical Contractor

Barnes Electrical Contracting is a full-service electrical contractor with extensive experience in both residential and commercial wiring. Established in 1986, we've grown to become the leading electrical contractor serving Colorado's north metro area.

Over the decades, we have built strong, lasting relationships with some of Colorado's finest general contractors. We provide electrical engineering, installation, and design services for commercial and multi-family projects, as well as residential work — delivering the unique combination of quality workmanship at a competitive price.

General contractors and developers across the Front Range trust us because they know they can rely on our services with complete confidence. That reputation is something we work hard to uphold every single day.
